---
name: Weekly stats choice
description: User intent for choosing projections versus season-to-date actual averages.
---

Keep season projections as the default for the weekly stats-basis choice. Let the user decide when to switch to current-season averages; do not automatically switch because the regular season has started.

**Why:** The user wanted to switch “at some point” and explicitly approved “Add the toggle, keeping projections as the default.”

**How to apply:** Preserve the user's saved selection when improving weekly forecasts, introducing new averaging windows, or changing early-season behavior. New statistical options must not silently change that choice or rewrite the draft's projection basis.
